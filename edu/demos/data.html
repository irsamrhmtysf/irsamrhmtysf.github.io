🗺 Data-driven creator roadmap — what successful creators actually do
01
Niche data analysis before first post
Top creators research 200–500 posts in their niche before publishing. They track: posting frequency of top accounts, engagement rate patterns, keyword gaps, and what gets comments (not just likes). Comments = deep engagement signal.
TubeBuddySemrushSocial Blade
02
A/B test thumbnails and titles — always
YouTube's algorithm gives a test window to every video. Changing the thumbnail in the first 48 hours to a higher-CTR version can 3× total views. Data: top YouTubers change thumbnails 4× per video on average.
CTR target: 6%+Top: 12-18%
03
Track retention, not just views
Average view duration matters more than total views for algorithm ranking. A video with 10k views at 70% retention outperforms 100k views at 15% retention. The data: 50%+ retention = algorithm boost. 70%+ = potential viral push.
Target: 50%+ retentionGreat: 70%+
04
Compound consistency — the data truth
Analysis of 10,000 creator accounts: channels that posted consistently for 12 months had 14× the subscribers of inconsistent channels with the same quality. Consistency beats quality in algorithmic visibility. This is not opinion — it's data.
Post 2-3×/week min12 months to traction

🌍 Life Expectancy vs GDP per capita
The pattern: Above $15k GDP per capita, life expectancy plateaus — after that, lifestyle choices matter more than wealth. Highest life expectancy: Japan (84.3), Switzerland (83.8), Singapore (83.5).
📊 Leading Causes of Death — Data vs Perception
Data surprises us: People fear plane crashes (1 in 11,000 lifetime risk) but ignore heart disease (1 in 5). AI health apps now use this data to personalise risk education — addressing what actually threatens you, not what feels scary.
