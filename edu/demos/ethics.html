Demographic Parity

The model must approve loans at the same rate for all groups, regardless of actual repayment probability.

P(approve | group A) = P(approve | group B)

Problem: If groups genuinely differ in repayment rates, this forces the model to ignore that signal — potentially disadvantaging profitable applicants in both groups.

Equalised Odds

Equal true positive AND false positive rates across groups. If you repay your loan, you should have the same chance of being approved regardless of group.

TPR_A = TPR_B AND FPR_A = FPR_B

Problem: Mathematically impossible to satisfy simultaneously with demographic parity unless base rates are equal (Chouldechova 2017).
