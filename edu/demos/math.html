Matrix multiplication A × B

The core operation of neural network forward passes. Each output element is a dot product of a row of A with a column of B.

×
=
C[i][j] = sum of A[i][k] × B[k][j] for all k
In a neural layer: output = W × input + bias

Why matrices matter in AI

A neural network layer with 784 inputs and 256 neurons computes:

output[256] = W[256×784] × input[784] + bias[256]

This single matrix multiply — evaluated on a GPU — processes one forward pass. With batch size 32:

output[32×256] = input[32×784] × W.T[784×256] + bias[256]
GPUs are fast at matrix multiplication because thousands of cores compute each C[i][j] in parallel. This is why GPU compute is the bottleneck for AI training.

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B) = P(B|A) × P(A) / P(B) — the foundation of probabilistic AI.

P(Disease | Test+) = P(Test+ | Disease) × P(Disease) / P(Test+)

Gaussian Distribution
