How Whisper Works

1 · Audio encoding

Audio is split into 30-second chunks and converted to log-Mel spectrograms — a visual representation of frequencies over time.

2 · Transformer encoder

A 12-layer Transformer encoder processes the spectrogram, learning to represent phonemes, words, and acoustic patterns.

3 · Autoregressive decode

A Transformer decoder generates the transcript token-by-token, conditioned on the audio encoding and all previously generated tokens.
